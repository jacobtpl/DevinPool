# Privacy Policy — Devin Pool (8 Ball Pool)

_Last updated: September 16, 2026_

Devin Pool does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- The app works entirely offline and makes no network requests.
- There are no user accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
- Game state exists only in memory on your device while the app is running.
- The app does not access the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or any other
  protected device data.

Because no data is collected, none is shared with third parties, sold, or used for tracking.

## Children

The app is rated 4+ and contains no content or features directed at collecting information
from children.

## Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy and the App Store privacy
labels will be updated before that version is released.
